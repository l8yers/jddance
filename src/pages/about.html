About JD Dance

JD Dance was established in 2016. We are a local dance school in Broughton where Salford borders Manchester. Our aim is to provide a safe space in which children can gain skills, have fun and grow confidence.

JD DANCE is a family which builds confidence through high quality, affordable dance classes for children from 2–16 years old. All of our teachers are DBS checked and have safeguarding training.
