Privacy notice

This notice explains how JD Dance uses information you provide when you contact us through this website.

Who to contact

For questions about your information, email jddanceex@gmail.com or contact JD Dance at Lockett St, Manchester, M8 8EF.

Information you send us

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, your area of interest and your message. We use these details to answer your enquiry, discuss availability and handle any booking you request.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to take steps at your request before a booking, or for our legitimate interest in responding to questions about JD Dance. Sending an enquiry does not sign you up for marketing.

How the form works

Our website and enquiry form use Netlify. Netlify processes form submissions on our behalf, and messages may also be handled through our email service. These providers may process information outside the UK; their applicable data protection arrangements govern those transfers. See Netlify’s privacy information and Google’s privacy information.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep enquiry messages only for as long as needed to respond and deal with the matter you contacted us about. If an enquiry leads to a booking, relevant information may need to be kept for administering that booking and applicable record-keeping obligations.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict its use, object to its use or request a portable copy. Contact us using the details above. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies and external links

This site does not include advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts and images are served by the site itself. Links to social media and maps take you to external services with their own privacy practices. Our hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website.
